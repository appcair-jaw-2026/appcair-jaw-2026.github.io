About the Workshop

The Joint APPCAIR Workshop brings the artificial intelligence research community across the BITS Pilani campuses together with researchers from other institutions and from industry. JAW 2026 is the second edition, hosted by APPCAIR at BITS Pilani, Pilani Campus on 14 and 15 August 2026.

Across two days the workshop presents invited research talks from academic and industry researchers, a panel discussion, industry lightning talks, a hackathon, a research quiz, and poster presentations by doctoral, postgraduate, and undergraduate students.

APPCAIR, the Anuradha and Prashanth Palakurthi Centre for Artificial Intelligence Research, conducts focused research in artificial intelligence across the BITS Pilani campuses and trains the next generation of researchers and data scientists.

Programme

Six threads run across the two days. The detailed schedule, with session timings, talk titles and abstracts, will be published on this page closer to the workshop.

Invited Research Talks

Talks from researchers at leading academic institutions and industry laboratories on their current work across artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Panel Discussion

A moderated panel putting academic and industry perspectives side by side on where AI research is heading and how it reaches the world.

Industry Lightning Talks

Short talks from industry practitioners on taking research into production, alongside stalls from participating organisations.

Hackathon

A hands-on build sprint running alongside the workshop. Theme, format, eligibility and prizes will be announced shortly.

Poster Presentations

Posters from doctoral, postgraduate and undergraduate students. The call for posters opens soon, with participation invited from across the campuses.

Research Quiz

A quiz on the history, ideas and practice of artificial intelligence, run from the main stage between sessions.
